Primary Research

Spatial Analysis of Motorcycle Fatality Trends in Malaysia

A comprehensive spatial examination of motorcycle fatality patterns across Malaysian regions from 2016 to 2024 — mapping distribution trends, identifying high-concentration zones, and informing national transport policy.

Road Safety Spatial Analytics Public Policy GIS Mapping

KEY FINDINGS

  • Fatality concentrations exhibit distinct spatial clustering along major urban corridors and intercity routes
  • Regional distribution patterns show significant variation between Peninsular and East Malaysian states
  • Temporal trends indicate seasonal fluctuation aligned with festive migration periods
  • Demographic analysis reveals differential distribution across age groups and license categories
