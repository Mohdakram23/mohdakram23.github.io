I.

About

I am a postgraduate researcher and teaching assistant at the Malaysia Institute of Transport (MITRANS), Universiti Teknologi MARA, completing my Master of Science in Transport and Logistics.

My work focuses on transport safety and public policy, using quantitative methods and open public data to support evidence-based decisions. I am drawn to research that translates careful analysis into real improvements in public outcomes.

In the classroom, I am committed to outcome-based teaching, careful assessment design, and the thoughtful use of AI to widen access to learning rather than narrow it. I write and teach in both Bahasa Malaysia and English.

“Good research should ultimately read like a careful argument for a better public decision.”
